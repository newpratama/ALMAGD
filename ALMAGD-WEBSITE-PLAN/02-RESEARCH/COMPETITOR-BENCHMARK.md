# COMPETITOR & PATTERN BENCHMARK

> Pattern-level benchmark from general industry knowledge of Puncak/Bogor villa & hotel websites, small Indonesian hospitality groups and international boutique hotel groups. **No factual claims about named competitors are made.**

## What typical Puncak villa/hotel sites do (and the gap we exploit)

| Pattern commonly seen | Problem | ALMAGD response |
|---|---|---|
| One-page sites or Instagram-only presence | Weak SEO, no trust | Structured multi-page site with schema & local SEO |
| Price lists that are outdated | Disputes, distrust | No room prices (DEC-007); WhatsApp for availability |
| WhatsApp number buried or one number for everything | Lost inquiries | Venue-specific WhatsApp with prefilled message (DEC-023) |
| Heavy sliders, auto-playing video, 5–10 MB pages | Slow on 4G in mountains | Performance budget (≤ 1 MB home) |
| Copied OTA reviews / fake testimonials | Legal & trust risk | Rating badges with source links only (DEC-011) |
| Generic stock photos | Expectation gap | Real photos only (PHOTOGRAPHY-DIRECTION) |
| Indonesian only | Misses Gulf & international guests | ID / EN / AR (DEC-012) |
| No map / wrong pin | Guests get lost in alleys | Per-venue map link + directions + landmarks |

## Patterns to adopt from good hospitality-group sites

1. Masterbrand header with a **collection grid** of venues on Home (clear portfolio).
2. Each venue page = mini-site: hero, overview, rooms, facilities, location, policies, book panel.
3. **Sticky mobile booking bar** (WhatsApp + Book) on venue pages.
4. Editorial photography with generous whitespace; restrained palette.
5. Location page with "Getting here" and nearby attractions.
6. Clear "Book on [platform]" outbound buttons instead of a fake booking form.
7. Menu as accessible HTML (not PDF), with categories and prices.

## Patterns to avoid
- Booking widgets that only redirect (confusing).
- Pop-ups on entry (newsletter, promo).
- Chat widgets loading third-party JS (use a simple WhatsApp link).
- Parallax / scroll-jacking.
- "Luxury", "5-star", "best in Puncak" claims.
