# PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

Shot list → [PHOTO-SHOT-LIST.md](../09-MEDIA/PHOTO-SHOT-LIST.md). Specs → [IMAGE-SPECS.md](../09-MEDIA/IMAGE-SPECS.md).

## Non-negotiables
1. **Real venue photos only.** No stock image may represent a venue, room or dish.
2. **No AI-generated or AI-"enhanced" venue images** (no sky replacement, no added furniture).
3. Owner must hold usage rights (own photos or written permission from photographer/OTA uploader).
4. Generic Puncak landscape images (tea plantation, mountains) are allowed **only** in Location/Explore sections, clearly not presented as the venue, and must be licensed.

## Style
| Aspect | Direction |
|---|---|
| Light | Natural; golden hour (06:00–08:00, 16:30–18:00) for exteriors & views; soft daylight for rooms; blue hour for night shots |
| Colour | Neutral-warm white balance, true greens, no heavy filters, no HDR halos |
| Composition | Eye-level, straight verticals (tilt-shift correction), leave negative space on one side for text overlays (heroes) |
| Rooms | Beds made, lights on, curtains open to view, no clutter, no personal items |
| People | Optional; candid families/couples from behind or side; signed model release |
| Food | Natural light, 45° and top-down, real portions, props from the cafe, steam/condensation real |
| Drone | Only with legal permission; establishing shots of setting/terrain |
| Night | Pool & building lit, city lights view (NP) |

## Editing
Exposure & white balance correction, lens correction, light sharpening. No object removal that misrepresents (e.g. removing neighbouring buildings).

## Delivery
Full-resolution JPEG (sRGB, ≥ 3000 px long edge for heroes, ≥ 2000 px others), filenames per [IMAGE-NAMING.md](../09-MEDIA/IMAGE-NAMING.md), with a rights note per batch.
