# BRAND VOICE

## Principles
1. **Clear before clever.** Short sentences, concrete nouns.
2. **Honest.** State facts plainly; never exaggerate.
3. **Warm.** Address the guest directly ("you" / "Anda" / "أنتم").
4. **Calm.** No exclamation marks in headlines; max one per page.

## Per language
| | Bahasa Indonesia | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|---|
| Register | Semi-formal, "Anda", friendly; avoid slang ("gokil", "cuan") | Plain international English (B1–B2) | Modern Standard Arabic, warm, family-respectful; avoid dialect |
| Sentence length | ≤ 20 words | ≤ 20 words | ≤ 22 words |
| Numbers | Rp 25.000; 14.00 | IDR 25,000; 2:00 pm | Latin digits (25,000 IDR / ‎14:00‎) — see MENU-CONTENT |
| Venue names | Never translated | Never translated | Never translated (Latin script kept) |

## Word lists
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| comfortable, cool, calm, view, welcome, family, together, clean, easy, highland | luxury, exclusive, world-class, best, cheapest, 5-star, paradise, hidden gem, must-visit |
| "Ask us on WhatsApp" | "Book now before it's gone!" |
| "Check availability" | "Limited rooms left" |

## Banned claims (unless owner provides proof)
Luxury · 5-star · best in Puncak · private pool · halal · award-winning · since [year] · eco-friendly · 24/7 room service.

## Sample rewrites
| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| "Experience ultimate luxury in Puncak's best villa!" | "Comfortable stays in the cool air of Puncak." |
| "Villa murah meriah, dijamin puas!!!" | "Menginap nyaman di udara sejuk Puncak." |
| "أفخم فيلا في بونشاك" | "إقامة مريحة في أجواء بونشاك العليلة." |
