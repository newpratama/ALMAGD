# TYPOGRAPHY DIRECTION

Implements DEC-026. Technical details → [TYPOGRAPHY.md](../08-DESIGN/TYPOGRAPHY.md).

| Role | Font | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Latin display (H1–H2, venue names) | **Fraunces** (500, 600) | Warm "soft serif" with character — premium without being cold or luxury-cliché; optical sizes keep it crisp |
| Latin body & UI | **Plus Jakarta Sans** (400–700) | Designed for Jakarta's identity — a quiet local story; excellent legibility; full Indonesian diacritics & Latin-ext |
| Arabic (all roles) | **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (400–700) | High-quality modern Naskh-influenced sans, very readable on mobile, harmonises with Plus Jakarta Sans proportions |

## Brand rules
- Venue names always in display font in Latin (also on Arabic pages, inside an LTR isolate).
- No all-caps for sentences; small caps eyebrow only for Latin (tracking +0.08em). Arabic never uses letter-spacing.
- Maximum 2 families loaded per locale page (Latin pages: Fraunces + Jakarta; Arabic pages: Plex Arabic + Jakarta for Latin fragments; Fraunces only for venue names if budget allows — otherwise Jakarta 600).
- All fonts OFL — free for commercial use, self-hosted.
