# VISUAL DIRECTION — "Highland Calm"

Implements DEC-025. System details → [DESIGN-DIRECTION.md](../08-DESIGN/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md).

## Mood in words
Early-morning mist over tea terraces · deep pine silhouettes · warm light in a wooden room · a cup of teh tarik on a balcony rail · quiet, orderly, welcoming.

## Visual ingredients
| Ingredient | Direction |
|---|---|
| Canvas | Warm off-white "mist" (#F6F3EC) — never pure grey, never stark white page backgrounds |
| Brand colour | Deep pine green (#1E3B32) for type, buttons, footer |
| Accent | Brass (#B8873B family) — thin rules, small icons, eyebrow text on dark; references "majd" (glory) subtly |
| Photography | Large, real, natural light; landscape crops; people optional and candid |
| Type | Elegant serif headlines (Fraunces) + clean humanist sans body (Plus Jakarta Sans); Arabic IBM Plex Sans Arabic |
| Shape | Soft radius (12px cards), no sharp boxes, no heavy shadows |
| Ornament | One motif: a thin brass horizontal line (32×2px) above eyebrows; optional subtle contour-line pattern (topographic) in footer at 6% opacity |
| Layout | Editorial: alternating image/text, wide margins, 1 idea per section |

## Avoid
- **Over-luxury:** black + gold, marble textures, script fonts, crown icons.
- **Generic template:** centered everything, stock icons in circles, 3-equal-column feature boxes for everything.
- **Cheap OTA look:** price stickers, red "DISKON" badges, star clutter, dense cards.
- **Artificial AI look:** generated "perfect" villas, over-saturated HDR skies, fake people.
- **Overloaded design:** sliders, many colours, gradients, emojis in UI.

## Reference adjectives for designers
Calm · grounded · crafted · airy · honest · warm.
