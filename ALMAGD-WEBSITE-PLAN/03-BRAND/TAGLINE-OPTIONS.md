## Recommended: **"Cool Air. Warm Welcome."** (DEC-005)
- **Truthful:** cool climate is a fact; guests repeatedly mention it (SRC-NJD-007).
- **Covers every venue** (stays and cafe) — not tied to pools or rooms.
- **Contrast device** (cool/warm) is memorable and works as a design motif (mist palette + brass accent).
- **Translates naturally**; Arabic version reads as poetic but plain.
- **Non-luxury**, fits DEC-006.

Descriptor line (SEO-friendly, used under tagline): EN "Villas, stays & dining in Puncak, Bogor" · ID "Villa, penginapan & kuliner di Puncak, Bogor" · AR "فلل وإقامة ومطعم في بونشاك، بوغور".

Runner-up for campaigns: #9 "Gather in the Cool of Puncak" (family/group campaigns).
