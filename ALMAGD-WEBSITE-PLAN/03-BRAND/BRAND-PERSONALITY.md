# BRAND PERSONALITY

| Trait | Is | Is not |
|---|---|---|
| Welcoming | Warm, hospitable, attentive | Over-familiar, pushy |
| Professional | Organised, accurate, clear | Corporate, stiff |
| Natural | Rooted in highland nature, calm | Rustic-cheap, "jungle" kitsch |
| Modern | Clean layouts, current | Trendy gimmicks |
| Trusted | Honest facts, real photos | Hype, fake scarcity |
| Family-friendly | Inclusive, safe, practical | Childish |
| Premium-feeling | Refined detail, quiet confidence | Luxury claims, gold overload |

**Archetype:** The Caregiver with a touch of The Everyman — "a reliable host who knows the mountain."

**If ALMAGD were a person:** a calm, well-dressed host who greets you by name, tells you the honest truth about the room, and knows where the best sunset is.
