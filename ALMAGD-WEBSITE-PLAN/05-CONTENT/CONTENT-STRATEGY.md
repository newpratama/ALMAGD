## Content principles
1. Facts from DATA-AUDIT only; placeholders otherwise.
2. Write Indonesian first (source), then English, then Arabic (TRANSLATION-PLAN).
3. Each section ≤ 60 words of body copy on mobile; details in accordions/dialogs.
4. Every page answers its visitor question above the fold or in the first scroll.
